How it learns

Every night the radar is rebuilt from fresh data. Then it grades itself.

  1. Measure. Searches that grew the most per country, with real monthly volumes from Google Ads over 25 months.
  2. Check now. Daily data up to today shows whether something is still rising or already over.
  3. Grade yesterday. What was on top yesterday is checked again today. Categories and sources that hold up count more tomorrow.
  4. Learn the noise. Words that keep turning out to be sports, news or celebrities are filtered out before they reach the list.
  5. Remember. Every topic keeps its first day on the radar, so you see what is new and what keeps coming back.
