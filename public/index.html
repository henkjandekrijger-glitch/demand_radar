Reading today's demand

How it works

Every night the radar measures which Google searches grew the most per country (DataForSEO, with Google Ads volumes over 25 months), checks with daily data whether they are still rising today, and adds what people type into Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo, Reddit and Hacker News wishes, today's trends, book and app charts, and a calendar of buying moments. AI sorts it into categories and removes sports, news and celebrities.

Then it grades itself: yesterday's top picks are checked again, and categories and sources that hold up count more the next day. Words that keep turning out to be noise are filtered automatically.

For teams and AI assistants: use the radar from your own tools.
