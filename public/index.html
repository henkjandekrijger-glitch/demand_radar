Building the map

Everything people practise for

Ranked by opportunity score: demand, growth, money, timing and the gap.

Reading 9 sources for every niche and country. The first time this takes up to two minutes

How the score works

0 to 100, built from five parts you can check in every row.

  • Demand · 35Monthly Google searches over all related terms (DataForSEO). Without that: how many distinct searches Google suggests, plus how many App Store ratings existing apps collected.
  • Growth · 20The last three months against the same months a year earlier. Without search data: Wikipedia interest year on year.
  • Money · 20What advertisers pay per click on Google. Three dollars or more counts as full.
  • Timing · 10A peak within ten weeks scores highest. Peaks come from two years of search data, from fixed exam and application periods, or from last year's Wikipedia pattern.
  • Gap · 15Few apps in the local language, a thin or outdated supply, low ad competition, unanswered questions, no recent books.

Sources

Google search suggestions (with question words), YouTube suggestions, Amazon search suggestions, App Store search (apps, ratings, languages, last update), Google Books, Google News, Wikipedia page views, and with DataForSEO: Google Ads search volumes for two years, cost per click, competition, related searches, and who ranks and advertises on Google.
