How it works

Free, live signals that move with buying demand. Every number links back to its source.

  • Google trending searchesWhat people in the Netherlands, the US and Germany suddenly search for, with estimated search counts and the news behind it. Refreshed every hour.
  • Wikipedia interestDaily page views for 80 tracked products and for every idea you check, with more than a year of history. This shows the trend now and the season ahead.
  • Global newsArticles from the last four weeks in the market's language, via GDELT. It catches what drives demand: new rules, subsidies, price jumps and shortages.
  • Demand calendarHolidays, shopping moments and regulation deadlines per market, with the date searches usually start rising.
  • What people typeGoogle, YouTube and Amazon search suggestions for your idea, nationally and with your city name. Amazon suggestions show buying intent.
  • ConversationsReddit posts and Hacker News stories from the last four weeks, counted per week.
  • Google NewsArticles in the market's language over the last four weeks, linked to the publisher.
  • WeatherSixteen-day forecasts from Open-Meteo for Amsterdam, Berlin, New York, Chicago and any city you check. Cold snaps, frost, heat and rain move demand.
  • Local businessesExisting places that match your idea within about 9 km of the city centre, from OpenStreetMap, with the count per 10,000 inhabitants.
  • Public holidaysOfficial days off in the Netherlands, Germany and the US, from Nager.Date.
  • Top chartsApp Store top free apps and Apple Books top paid books per country.
  • AI analystOn Pro + AI: reads all of the above and researches the web for your idea, then gives a verdict, risks and a first step.

Questions

Is this Google search volume?

No. Exact search volumes come from paid sources. Demand Radar combines free, live signals that move with search demand: Google's trending searches, Wikipedia interest and news. That makes it fast and fresh, and strong on timing. Exact search volumes are on the roadmap.

How fresh is the data?

Trending searches and spikes refresh every hour. Wikipedia interest is counted per day. News is searched live when you check an idea.

Which markets?

The Netherlands, the United States, Germany and a worldwide English view.

Can I stop?
