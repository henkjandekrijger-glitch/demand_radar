Questions

How accurate is it?

We test the forecast on the past: the model learns from data up to a month and is graded on the months after. Of the rises it predicted for May to September 2026, 94% rose by more than 10% and 90% by 40% or more. Every prediction shows its own chance, and that chance matched what happened.

Where does the data come from?

Monthly Google search volumes (four years per search), Google Trends, weather, economic indicators, school holidays and news. Search data comes through DataForSEO. We never use personal data to forecast.

How is this different from Exploding Topics?

Local, measured searches for the Netherlands and Germany (also per city) instead of global trends; a published, tested hit rate; a chance per month for the next 1 to 4 months; and advice on what to do: when to start, what a test costs and cheaper search terms.

Can I cancel?
